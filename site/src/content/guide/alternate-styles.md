MC3DPrint ships two optional looks for every machine, item, and screen. They're built into the mod jar, so there's nothing extra to download, and they only restyle MC3DPrint's own content — your other resource packs are untouched.

- **Blueprint Mode** — every machine drawn as its own schematic: blueprint blues and white technical line-work. The mod's signature look.
- **Dark Mode** — matte near-black chassis; the cyan glow and tier accent colors stay loud.

Both styles keep gameplay information intact: tier colors stay distinguishable at a glance, silhouettes stay recognizable, and GUI layouts never move.

## Enabling a style

1. From the title screen or pause menu, open **Options → Resource Packs**.
2. Find **MC3DPrint: Blueprint Mode** or **MC3DPrint: Dark Mode** in the left (Available) column.
3. Hover the pack and click the **arrow** to move it to the right (Selected) column.
4. Click **Done**. Resources reload and the new look applies immediately.

Move the pack back to the left column at any time to return to the default art.

## Standalone downloads

Every release also attaches each style as a standalone zip (`mc3dprint-style-<name>-<version>.zip`) for players who prefer managing packs themselves:

1. Download the zip from the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/PGMacDesign/MC3DPrint/releases).
2. Drop it into `.minecraft/resourcepacks/` (don't unzip it).
3. Enable it in **Options → Resource Packs** as above.

One zip covers every supported Minecraft version. Use either the built-in pack or the zip, not both at once.
