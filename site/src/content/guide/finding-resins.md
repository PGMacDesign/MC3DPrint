The strongest resins can't be crafted at all. **Rare**-tier resins are loot-only, and two effects, **Quartermaster** and **Ore Salting**, exist *only* at Rare, so exploring is the sole way to get them.

## Where they hide

Each qualifying chest has a small chance to hold one random Rare resin. Look in end-game structures:

- End cities
- Ancient cities
- Nether fortresses & bastions
- Woodland mansions
- Strongholds
- Buried treasure

## Make it count

They're scarce on purpose. Pair a hard-won resin with a hard-won blueprint for the best print of your life. See [what resins do](/guide/resin-effects/) for the full effect list.
