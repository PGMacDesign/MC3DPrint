Tiers 1 through 4 are single-block printers, your bread-and-butter machines for turning a written disc into a real build. They run on Redstone Flux pulled from any side.

## What each tier can do

- **Tier 1–2** print items only.
- **Tier 3** unlocks structure printing with a 3×3 footprint.
- **Tier 4** grows that to 5×5.

Higher tiers also print faster, cost less FU per block, and accept more [upgrade modules](/guide/upgrades/): one slot per tier, so a Tier 4 has four. They unlock higher material tiers too: a Tier 3 machine can't print a Tier 6 (netherite) block, so match your printer to what you're building.

> Losing power only pauses a job: progress is never lost, and the print resumes the moment power returns.

## Crafting your first printer

The Tier 1 printer comes together from a piston, a little redstone, and an iron shell. It's your first machine and your gateway into the whole loop: scan, save, print.

## Remote Terminal

Sneak-click a printer with the Remote Terminal to pair the two, then place the terminal anywhere in your base. Right-click it to open that printer's GUI from across the room. No walking back to the machine.

## Going bigger

When a 5×5 footprint stops cutting it, Tiers 5 through 8 are multiblock [fabricators](/guide/fabricators/) built from an N×N frame. They print the largest structures and burn through the most FU. See [the FU economy](/guide/fu-economy/) for how cost scales.
