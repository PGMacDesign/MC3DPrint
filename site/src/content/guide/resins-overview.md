A Resin is a one-shot additive that makes a blueprint print come out finer: better, not just bigger. Drop one into the `Resin` slot, between the upgrade and spool columns, before you print.

## How resins work

- A resin only works on **found** blueprints: looted or creative discs, never your own scans. That's the anti-dupe rule.
- It's consumed **per print**, but only when it has something to do. Slot a Treasure resin with no chests to fill and the printer leaves it untouched.

> Pair a hard-won resin with a hard-won blueprint for the best print of your life.

## Rarity

Resins come in three rarities. Rarer is stronger, and a few effects exist only at the top.

- **Common** and **Uncommon** are craftable.
- **Rare** is found only, in end-game loot. See [finding rare resins](/guide/finding-resins/).

## Crafting

Everything starts with **Resin Base**: an Extrudium Crystal plus any slimeball. From there:

1. Add a **rarity ingredient** (a gem bumps it to Uncommon).
2. Add an **effect ingredient** to finish the resin.

See [what resins do](/guide/resin-effects/) for the full effect list.
