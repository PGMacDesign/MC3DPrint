One wire, two cargos. The MC3D Cable carries both Redstone Flux and Filament Units down a single line. It connects automatically to printers, fabricators, [Filament Racks](/guide/filament-rack/), generators, and any other mod's RF machines. No configuring faces.

## Power

The cable moves standard Forge Energy, but modestly: **2,000 FE/t** (tunable in config). That's plenty to run this mod's machines, and you can use it as a general-purpose power wire for any mod too.

> It is deliberately **not** an endgame power backbone. For serious throughput, bring a dedicated cable.

## Filament Units

FU is pulled on demand. A printer wired to one or more racks drains them the instant its docked spools run dry, so a single rack can feed a whole row of machines. Transfer is **lossless**: no FU lost to distance.

## Crafting

Cheap to craft, and one batch yields 6, enough to wire up a small machine row from a single craft. Pair it with a [Filament Rack](/guide/filament-rack/) reservoir to keep your printers fed.
